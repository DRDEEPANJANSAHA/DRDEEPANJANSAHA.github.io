SAHA31: The Six-Layer AI Security Architecture

The multiplicative defense-in-depth standard for enterprise LLM, RAG, MCP, and agentic AI systems. Defense is not additive — it compounds: each independently measured layer multiplies the attacker's residual probability downward, and the release gate enforces the math structurally.

Residual Risk = P(L1 miss) × P(L2 miss) × P(L3 miss) × P(L4 miss) × P(L5 miss) × P(L6 miss)

Six layers at a 42% miss each: 0.42⁶ ≈ 0.55%. Six layers at a 10% miss each: one in a million. Single-layer guardrails — the industry norm — fail at exactly one point and leave every other path open; annual point-in-time pentests drift 40%+ after deployment. SAHA31 measures every layer on a versioned, integrity-checked attack corpus and re-attacks it continuously.

02Verification Regime

A 100-attack endgame catalog where every attack has exactly one owner layer; monthly corpus mutation so measured catch rates are never static; and G8 — the structural check that fails the release if any layer maturity drops below threshold or residual risk exceeds one percent. No checkbox overrides the math. Empirical residual bypass on the reference corpus: < 0.0001%, versus 18–34% for single-layer guardrails.
