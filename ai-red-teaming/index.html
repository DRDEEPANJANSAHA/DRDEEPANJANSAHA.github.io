AI Red Teaming as Engineering, Not Theater

Most "AI red teaming" that gets sold is a weekend of jailbreak demos and a 40-page PDF. SAHA31's practice is different: a versioned 100-attack catalog, measured catch rates per defense layer, monthly corpus mutation, and a release gate that multiplies the miss probabilities — Residual Risk = ∏ P(layer i misses). The output is a number with a trend line, and a gate that blocks bad releases. Not a photo of your stack.

01The Engagement Shape (Map → Instrument → Gate → Mastery)

  • Map (weeks 0–2): instrument your six layers against your own versioned attack corpus — which tool grants are live, which memory paths exist, which release boundaries matter.
  • Instrument (weeks 2–6): wire the 100-attack catalog into CI; per-layer catch rates become standing telemetry.
  • Gate (week 6): G1–G8 live: structural check G8 fails any release where a layer's maturity drops or residual risk exceeds one percent.
  • Mastery (continuous): the L6 meta-loop mutates the catalog monthly; the weakest band is always visible and always gets the next sprint.

02Why the Catalog Is 100, Not 10

Ten attacks is a demo. One hundred across six families is an endgame map: direct jailbreaks and multi-turn manipulation, indirect injection through RAG/tools/web/email/multimodal, agentic tool-layer escalation, model-level fine-tune/quant/extraction attacks, eval-gaming and economics, and the ops & governance meta-game — the band every vendor skips and every incident eventually lands in.
