Cross-Tenant Retrieval and Data Bleed

Indirect & Retrieval · L2/L4 · RAG + memory · Indirect & Retrieval

Multi-tenant stores that leak a neighbor's records on retrieval are both a confidentiality breach and an injection channel: your tenant's agent starts reasoning over someone else's data. Isolation that holds in tests but not at scale is the usual failure.

01Control Architecture

How it is defended

Tenant isolation is a hard partition, tested with cross-tenant canaries in CI. A bleed detected in test fails the release; bleed in production is an incident, not a finding.

03FAQ

What is cross-tenant bleed in RAG systems?

One tenant's retrievals returning another tenant's records — confidentiality loss plus a live injection channel. Hard partitioning, per-tenant canaries in CI, and release gates on any detected bleed are the standing controls.

How do you defend against cross-tenant retrieval and data bleed?

Tenant isolation is a hard partition, tested with cross-tenant canaries in CI. A bleed detected in test fails the release; bleed in production is an incident, not a finding.
